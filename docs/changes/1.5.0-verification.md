Known limits behind ⚠️: on aarch64, PyMOL (x86_64 only), SCINE/ORCA (no
aarch64 wheels) and Vina (needs Boost to build); mlip/fairchem on aarch64
need glibc ≥ 2.34, so older hosts use the container images.

## Not covered by 1.5.0 testing

- macOS and Windows (unsupported; the launcher refuses with a message).
- The generative stack on x86_64 (conda only; follow-up).
- Hosts with NVIDIA drivers older than 580 (CUDA 13 builds) — expected to fail
  on GPU; to be confirmed if desta or Engaging has one.
